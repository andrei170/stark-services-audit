Quickstrike Media handle your site and your SEO for $1,200 a month, and it is working. We are not proposing to rebuild anything, replace them, or get involved in that lane. There is no website phase in this plan and no build fee.

The Problem, In One Line
Read from your live Tag Manager container, 4 September 2026
Container GTM-PGDXJN2W Google Ads account AW-17972349451 Conversion tags 7 firing Conversion linkers 5 present Value per conversion "100" — every single one

Your tracking is not broken. It is well built. Seven conversion tags, properly linked — that puts you ahead of almost every contractor we look at.

The problem is that all seven report the same number. A $600,000 commercial build and an $8,000 repair both arrive at Google as a $100 conversion.

So Google optimises for neither. It has no way to know which enquiries you actually want, which means it buys whichever clicks are cheapest — and the cheapest clicks in construction are almost never the big jobs.

Everything in this plan follows from fixing that one signal.

Why Option 1 is the recommendation, and it is not about the money. Value-based bidding needs conversion volume before Google's algorithm can learn anything at all. The small jobs are what teach the system to recognise the big ones. Starting commercial-first means paying for expensive clicks while the account has nothing to learn from. Option 2 works, it just takes longer to get honest data out of.

The Meta Alternative — And An Honest Assessment
You asked what Meta would look like. Here it is, including where it falls down.
AG1 management fee $1,250/mo Your ad spend $1,250/mo paid to Meta, not to us Total out of pocket $2,500/mo

What Meta is genuinely good at for a business like yours:

  • Your "mailbox money" work. Modular home pads and quick residential jobs are impulse-adjacent and visual — that is exactly what Meta sells well.
  • Showing finished work. Construction is one of the most visual trades there is, and before-and-after content performs on Facebook in a way it never will on a search results page.
  • Being known in a small market. Palestine and Anderson County are small enough that sustained local presence compounds — people recognise the name before they need you.
  • Your background. An IT and cybersecurity man running a construction company is an unusual story, and Meta is where a story like that can actually be told.

And where it will not deliver:

Meta will not bring you $350,000 commercial projects. Nobody scrolling Facebook decides to commission a six-figure build. That work is won on bids, relationships and referrals — and the honest position is that no advertising platform buys it directly. What advertising does is get you known and get you invited.

So we would not recommend Meta instead of Google. We would recommend it after — once the Google side is producing and there is budget to build presence alongside it.

If you wanted to start with Meta anyway, it is the cheaper entry at $2,500 and it would fill the small-job pipeline. It just would not move you toward the thing you actually said you wanted, which is bigger and longer projects.

One Thing To Revisit
Remarketing — your call, but worth a second look

You have remarketing switched off because you find it annoying personally. That is a completely fair position and we are not going to argue you out of it for consumer-style retargeting.

But a $350,000 project has a decision cycle measured in months, and during that time the buyer is comparing you against two or three others. Remarketing on the commercial side is not chasing someone round the internet — it is being visible while a slow decision is being made.

Our suggestion: leave it off for the small-job campaigns entirely, and consider it for commercial only. Different job, different intent.
