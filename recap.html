Your Own County — Anderson County
Four local firms. Not one of them advertises anywhere.
  • Bridges & Sons, CJ Enterprises, 12 Point Construction, Mud Rock — zero on Google, zero on Meta, zero Meta pixels. Two of the four have no enquiry form at all.
  • CJ Enterprises has been in Anderson County twenty-plus years on word of mouth and the sign on the truck. That is not a weakness of theirs, it is the shape of the opportunity.
  • Mud Rock is the one to learn from — no advertising, but the site leans hard on reviews. They know proof is what sells this work.
  • You already have seven working conversion tags, analytics and a form. Nobody else in either table has that combination.

The Five Things Worth Remembering
  • Everybody is selling clearing. Nobody is selling pads. All eleven Meta advertisers lead with land clearing or forestry mulching. Pads, site prep, drainage and commercial or agricultural work: zero out of eleven.
  • You are the best-equipped firm in the market and the only one not switched on. They turned their ads on without building the tracking. You built the tracking and never turned the ads on.
  • The flat $100 conversion value is the first fix. Every enquiry reports the same, so a pad and a twenty-acre clear look identical to the algorithm. It costs nothing to change.
  • Bronco is the only real threat. Tyler is a different market today. If they start bidding on Anderson County searches, it stops being one.
  • Four of the seven things every advertiser does are free. Your name in the ad, insured and licensed stated, a dated offer, reviews in the headline. Video and multiple cuts are the two that cost real money, and neither is optional.
The Order We Would Do It In
Straight out of the three documents
  • 1. Fix the flat $100 conversion value.
  • 2. Pads and site prep, told as one real job with the price in it.
  • 3. Video, with more than one cut.
  • 4. The four free things — your name, insured and licensed, a dated offer, your reviews.
  • 5. A Meta pixel.
  • Then drainage into the autumn, land clearing only once something is working and never on price, commercial and agricultural site work, and owning Anderson County before Bronco does.
One correction worth knowing

Our first Google analysis compared you with RLM, KEA and Boone & Boone. Those are commercial general contractors who win work from bid lists, and they are not who you lose jobs to. The Anderson County and East Texas firms above are the corrected set.
