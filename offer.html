Option B

No Ceiling, Shared Upside

$3,000/mo + 1.5% of sales we bring in
ad spend uncapped · scaled up while it keeps producing jobs
  • $3,000/mo management fee — the same as Option A
  • 1.5% of the sale value of jobs we bring in, agreed and tracked in writing
  • No cap on ad spend. When a campaign is producing booked jobs, we keep putting money behind it instead of stopping at a monthly limit
  • Ad spend still paid by you, direct to Google — we take no percentage of it
  • The same rebuilt tracking, campaigns and reporting as Option A
  • You can pause or pull spend back at any time
Best if the goal is as many of the big jobs as the market will give you.

Why We Would Offer Option B At All
A cap is the wrong shape for work this size

With a hard monthly limit, a campaign that is finding commercial projects stops on the day the budget runs out — even if the next click is a $350,000 build. Option B lets the spend follow the results, and the 1.5% means we only earn more when you have actually sold more.

It also keeps us honest in the other direction: a campaign that is not producing booked jobs does not get more money. Spend goes up because the numbers justify it, not because there is budget left.

The ground rules for Option B, agreed in writing before we start
  • What counts as "a job we bring in" — tracked from a campaign click, call or form to a signed job.
  • Sale value means the signed contract value, and the 1.5% is due when you are paid, not when you quote.
  • Referrals, repeat customers and bid work you win yourself are not included.
  • A monthly review of spend against booked jobs, so any increase is a decision you can see.
  • You stay in control of the card. Ad spend is always yours and can be paused at any time.
